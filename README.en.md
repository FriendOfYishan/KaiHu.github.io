# Kai Hu's research blog

[中文](README.md) · [English](README.en.md)

An optimizer research retrospective: **51 main identifiers plus 9 branch/engineering notes**, each available in Chinese and English. Every note covers its predecessor, change, result or limitation, and next question. Compiled on 2026-11-21.

- [English research index](https://friendofyishan.github.io/KaiHu.github.io/research/en/)
- [中文研究目录](https://friendofyishan.github.io/KaiHu.github.io/research/zh/)
- [Attachment catalog](_assets/optimizer-journey/catalog.json)

Small attachments retain a core implementation or mathematical check, a principal report, and a few small evidence files. Each README states dependencies, the minimal command and reproduction scope. SOURCE_MANIFEST.json records provenance and source hashes. Datasets, checkpoints, remote-control helpers and vendored dependencies are omitted.

Some identifiers are theory/evidence audits, not separately trained algorithms. Algebra checks, synthetic optimizer updates and full historical training reproduction are distinguished. Reports remain in Chinese; the English posts provide concise research accounts. The record explicitly preserves implementation differences in 47, the long-budget quality limits of 51, and the unfinished full FineWeb comparison.

Built with Jekyll and Minima. Language navigation uses ordinary pages and requires no multilingual plugin. `_assets` is explicitly included in the site build. Update `url` and `baseurl` if moving to an account-root site or custom domain.
